# German learning

German vocabulary and translation practice for a Spanish-speaking learner.

## Language

**Vocabulary entry**:
A word or expression with a meaning in one language, a learning level and optional grammatical forms.

**Study card**:
One direction of recalling a vocabulary entry or phrase. Recognizing German and producing German are separate cards.

**Lifetime mistakes**:
The number of recorded recall failures on a study card, including imported Anki failures. Going back or undoing a review does not erase a mistake.
_Avoid_: Difficulty score

**Active difficulty**:
The remaining need to reinforce a word, combining its recall failures with credit earned through completed Writing exercises. New failures raise it; reinforcement lowers it.
_Avoid_: Lifetime mistakes

**Writing reinforcement**:
One completed translation check for an exercise containing a target word. Rechecking that same exercise is still the same reinforcement.

**CEFR level**:
An estimated difficulty of vocabulary or an exercise, from A1 to C2. Vocabulary subdivisions distinguish the earlier and later halves of a band.
